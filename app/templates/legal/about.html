About UWA Marketplace

A peer-to-peer marketplace built by students, for students — making campus life a little easier and a lot more sustainable.

Our Mission

UWA Marketplace was created to give students a trusted, simple way to buy and sell goods within the UWA community. Whether you're offloading last semester's textbooks or hunting for affordable furniture before move-in day, we're here to connect you with fellow students.

Why We Built This

Students spend a lot on study materials that end up unused after semester ends. UWA Marketplace reduces waste, saves money, and builds community — all at once. It's the circular economy, made simple for campus life.
