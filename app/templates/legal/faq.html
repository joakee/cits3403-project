Getting Started

UWA Marketplace is open to current UWA students, staff, and alumni. You'll need to register with a valid email address. We recommend using your UWA student email for faster community trust.

Yes — completely free. There are no listing fees, transaction fees, or premium plans. UWA Marketplace is a student project built to benefit the community.

After registering, we send a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) to your email. Enter it on the verification page to activate your account. OTPs expire after 10 minutes — use the "Resend" option if needed, but wait at least 60 seconds between requests. Make sure to check your spam folder.

Buying

Open any listing and click the Message Seller button. This opens a private chat between you and the seller through the UWA Marketplace messaging system. You'll receive a notification when they reply.

No. UWA Marketplace is a listing and communication platform only. Payment and handover arrangements are made directly between buyer and seller. We recommend using safe payment methods like bank transfer, PayID, or cash for in-person meetups.

Click the heart icon on any listing card or listing detail page. You'll be prompted to choose an existing wishlist or create a new one. Manage all your wishlists from your profile page.

Selling

Log in, then click List Item in the navigation bar. Fill in the title, description, price, category, and optionally upload a photo. Your listing will appear immediately in the marketplace.

The following are prohibited:
  • Weapons, dangerous goods, or controlled substances
  • Counterfeit, stolen, or pirated items
  • Adult content or services
  • Anything illegal under Australian law
Violations will result in listing removal and potential account suspension.

Yes. Navigate to any of your listings and click Edit Listing. You can update the title, description, price, photo, and other details. You can also deactivate or delete a listing from the same page.

Safety & Trust

Use the Report button on any listing or profile. Our moderation team reviews all reports and takes appropriate action, which may include listing removal or account suspension.

  • Meet in public, well-lit areas on campus (e.g. library foyer, student guild).
  • Inspect items before paying.
  • Use PayID or bank transfer — avoid cash for high-value items.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, don't proceed.
  • Keep communication within the UWA Marketplace messaging system.

Go to Profile → Settings and scroll to the danger zone section. Account deletion is permanent and will remove all your listings, messages, and personal data within 30 days.
