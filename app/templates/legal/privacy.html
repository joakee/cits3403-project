1. Information We Collect

When you use UWA Marketplace, we may collect the following types of information:

Information You Provide

  • Account data: Name, email address, and password hash.
  • Profile data: Optional bio, profile photo, and store details.
  • Listing data: Item titles, descriptions, prices, and photos you upload.
  • Messages: Chat messages exchanged with other users through the Platform.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Session identifiers and authentication tokens.
  • Browser type, IP address, and approximate location (for security purposes).
  • Pages visited and actions taken on the Platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Display your listings and profile to other users.
  • Send transactional emails such as OTP verification codes.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • Improve Platform functionality and user experience.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and community guidelines.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, nor do we use it for targeted advertising.

3. Sharing of Information

We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Your username, listings, and profile are visible to other registered users.
  • Service providers: We use third-party email services to deliver system emails. These providers are contractually prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to a valid legal request.
  • Safety: We may share information to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

4. Cookies & Session Data

UWA Marketplace uses cookies and session storage for:

  • Authentication: Keeping you logged in across page visits.
  • CSRF protection: Security tokens to prevent cross-site request forgery.
  • Theme preference: Remembering your light/dark mode selection.

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect Platform functionality.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on servers located in Australia. We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms (PBKDF2-SHA256).
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Rate limiting and brute-force protection on authentication endpoints.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data and listings are removed within 30 days. Some anonymised data may be retained for analytics and platform improvement purposes.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable Australian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at marketplace@uwa.edu.au.

8. Children's Privacy

UWA Marketplace is not intended for users under the age of 18 without parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via the Platform or email. Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out:

marketplace@uwa.edu.au
